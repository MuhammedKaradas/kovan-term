# Code of conduct

Kovan Term follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions, experiences and ways of working.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [MuhammedKaradas](https://github.com/MuhammedKaradas), through the
contact on that GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow
this code. They may also warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
